Horizon web

Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3 rewritten in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly and drawn with WebGPU. It plays on the resources prepared from your own copy of the game.

    Your prepared resources
    Drop a zip of the folder horizon-prepare made (.local/horizon-client/26.3, or resources/ in a release), or pick it.
    Options

    Saves, options and worlds stay in this browser (IndexedDB). ·
